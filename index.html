AIP Conference Proceedings, 2026

Family Planning Decisions and Child Investment Patterns: Evidence from Malaysian Families

This preliminary study used questionnaire data from Malaysian families and regression analysis to examine family size decisions and investment in children’s education, health and development. It found no statistically significant relationship between child quality and child quantity in the sample. Investment in child quality was associated with household income, health expenditure and family planning satisfaction, while child quantity was associated with parental age. Parents also identified financial stability, access to education and healthcare, and personal readiness as considerations in family size decisions. The findings highlight the importance of context-specific family policies.

Khazanah Research Institute : Research Publication

A Quick Look at the KL Residential Rental Affordability Market

Using salary and rental price data, this article examines whether renting in Kuala Lumpur is affordable, amid a housing supply trend towards condominiums and serviced apartments. Applying the Housing Cost Burden (HCB) benchmark of no more than 30% of household income spent on housing, it finds that an individual earning an average salary is likely to struggle to rent affordably. The article argues that housing policy should support affordable renting alongside home ownership, making both viable housing options.

RekodJa : Automated Job Tracker

Chrome Extension & Web App
Independent project · Product requirements, AI-assisted development & testing
101extension installs

As of 29 September 2026

The Problem

Job hunting involves tracking dozens of applications across diverse platforms. Manual spreadsheet logging is slow, fragmented, and prone to abandonment.

The Solution

Two interconnected tools sharing one single Google Sheet source: save live job details instantly via browser extension, then analyze and manage follow-ups via the web dashboard.

  • Automated DOM Parsing: Dynamically extracts role title, hiring company, date, and link directly from active job boards, eliminating manual data entry.
  • Direct Cloud Persistence: Authenticates via OAuth 2.0 to write rows straight into the candidate's personal Google Sheet.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: The extension writes directly to your selected Google Sheet. The web app uses Supabase for application data and account features.
  • Session & Dashboard Analytics: Secure Google OAuth session handling with visual breakdowns of application velocity, response rates, and interview pipeline stages.
  • Bidirectional Synchronization: Imports applications from a connected Google Sheet and writes stage changes back, with feedback when synchronization fails.
  • Gmail Integration Engine: Optional read-only Gmail scans identify application emails and suggest or automatically apply eligible status updates.

Econ Tutor : Interactive Economics Learning

Web App
Independent project · Economics learning from SPM to undergraduate level
20active users

As of 30 September 2026

The Problem

Economics learners, from SPM to undergraduate level, often work with static diagrams and learning materials scattered across textbooks and PDFs. These make it harder to explore how economic relationships change.

The Solution

An interactive economics learning site for SPM, STPM, Matriculation and undergraduate-level learning, bringing together notes, draggable graphs and calculators, with flashcards, quizzes and trial-paper practice for SPM.
